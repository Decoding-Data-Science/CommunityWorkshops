        How we teach AI
        
The Four-Layer Shift

        
Our core teaching framework. Teams move from writing better prompts to building AI systems that run, check and improve themselves. Each programme starts at the layer your team is ready for.

      

      

        
Layer 1
Prompt Engineering
Clear instructions, examples and output formats that get reliable answers from any AI assistant.

        
Layer 2
Context Engineering
Grounding AI in your documents, tables and policies so answers reflect your organisation, not the internet.

        
Layer 3
Harness Engineering
Tools, guardrails, evaluation and logging around the model so it can act safely inside real workflows.

        
Layer 4
Loop Engineering
Agents and pipelines that run on schedule, measure their own results and improve with feedback.

        Capstone projects
        
What teams build by the end

        
Representative capstone briefs from our sector sandboxes. Your team's capstone is shaped around the problem you bring.

      

      

        
Security Operations
After-hours access monitor
Flags logins outside working hours, repeated MFA failures and access that remains after an account is disabled, with a daily summary written by an LLM.
Databricks · SQL · LLM summary

        
Human Resources
Attrition early-warning dashboard
Combines tenure, leave patterns and grade movement to show which teams are at risk, and why, before resignations arrive.
Python · Dashboards · Copilot slides

        
Telecom
Complaint triage assistant
Classifies free-text tickets by category and urgency, links them to network KPIs and routes the worst sites to engineering.
OpenAI API · Classification · SQL

        
Energy & Plant
Predictive maintenance alerts
Tracks vibration and temperature drift on pumps and compressors and raises a work order before an unplanned shutdown.
PySpark · Time series · Alerts

        
Finance & Procurement
Duplicate invoice detector
Finds near-identical invoices across vendors and amounts, and explains each match in plain language for the approver.
Data quality · Fuzzy matching · LLM

        
Public Services
SLA breach predictor
Predicts which service requests will miss their target and groups citizen feedback into themes in Arabic and English.
Databricks · ML · Text themes
